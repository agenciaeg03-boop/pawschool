This Privacy Policy explains how ("we", "us", "our") collects and uses personal information when you visit our pages, buy PawSchool or PawSchool Fast Track, or receive emails from us.

1. Information we collect

  • Purchase information: your name, email address and order details. Card payments are processed by our payment provider. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Email information: your email address and whether you opened or clicked our emails, if our email provider reports it.
  • Support messages: anything you send us when you contact support.
  • Training progress: your pet's profile, lesson progress, XP, badges and training log are saved only in your own browser on your device. This data is not sent to us.
  • Technical data: our hosting and payment providers may log basic data such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, for security and to keep the service working.

2. How we use it

  • To deliver your purchase and give you access
  • To send receipts, access details and important account messages
  • To send training tips and offers, if you have not unsubscribed

  • To prevent fraud and meet legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers who help us run the business, such as our payment processor, email provider and hosting provider, and only as needed for them to do that work. We may also disclose information when required by law.

4. How to unsubscribe from emails

You can stop receiving marketing emails from us at any time, for free. Use either option:

  1. One click: open any marketing email from PawSchool, scroll to the bottom and click "Unsubscribe". You will be removed from our marketing list.
  2. By email: send a message to with the subject line "Unsubscribe" from the address you want removed.

We process unsubscribe requests within 10 business days. After you unsubscribe, you may still receive transactional emails about your purchase, such as receipts, access details or refund confirmations. Unsubscribing does not cancel your access to PawSchool.

5. Canceling, refunds and subscriptions

PawSchool and PawSchool Fast Track are one-time payments, not subscriptions. You will not be charged again, so there is no recurring plan to cancel.

If you want to cancel your purchase, you can request a full refund within 60 days of purchase under our 60-Day Money-Back Guarantee. Email with the subject line "Refund request". Full details are in our Terms of Service.

6. Deleting your training data

Because your training progress lives only in your browser, you control it directly. To delete it, open the Progress tab in PawSchool and choose "Reset all progress", or clear your browser's site data.
